About Moonrise

AI close agents for mid-market controllers.

Join our founding account executive to sell close and reconciliation agents to controllers and CFOs at mid-market companies.

What you'll do

  • Run full sales cycles from discovery to signature with finance leaders on NetSuite, Sage Intacct and QuickBooks Online
  • Build pipeline with outbound, partners, events and customer referrals
  • Work with our solutions engineer through security reviews and proofs of concept
  • Help shape the playbook, pricing and forecasting as the team grows

What you bring

  • 3+ years closing B2B SaaS, ideally selling to finance or accounting teams
  • A record of creating your own pipeline and hitting quota
  • Comfort talking about close, reconciliations and audit with a controller
  • Coachability and follow-through

Nice to have

  • Selling into NetSuite or Sage Intacct customers
  • Accounting or audit background

On-target earnings are twice the base salary, with commission paid under a written plan. Hybrid in San Jose or remote in California.

Pay and benefits

Pay range: $130,000 to $160,000 base salary. This is a good-faith estimate of what we expect to pay.

Plus variable pay (commission) targeted at 100% of base under a written plan.

Equity: stock options of 0.08% to 0.25% of fully diluted shares, 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff, then monthly.

Benefits: medical, dental and vision coverage, 15 days of vacation a year, California paid sick leave and 11 paid company holidays.

We never ask about salary history.

Hiring practices

We are not able to sponsor work visas for this role.

Offers depend on a background check, which runs only after a conditional offer.

Moonrise will consider qualified applicants with arrest and conviction records in a manner consistent with the California Fair Chance Act. We ask about criminal history only after a conditional offer.
